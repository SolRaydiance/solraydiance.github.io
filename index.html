03Our concept

Sunlight, transformed

Introducing
HelioPure.

A proposed solar-thermal water purification system designed to use concentrated sunlight for water distillation.

A dish reflector concentrates solar energy onto a receiver, generating heat that can be used to evaporate water. The resulting vapour is condensed and collected as purified water.

Beyond HelioPure

Upcoming

01Upcoming

HelioPure-As-A-Service

SolRaydiance can establish water purification outlets, positioning HelioPure as both a service and a product. Customers can pay to purify their water without purchasing the machine, while HelioPure remains available for standalone sale.

02Upcoming

HelioVolt

HelioVolt is a proposed steam-powered electricity generator that converts steam pressure and kinetic energy into rotational motion to drive a turbine and generate electricity. Its future integration with HelioPure could enable combined water purification and power generation.
